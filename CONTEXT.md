# Mate

Mate coordinates delegated work for a human Chef, with the care of a Michelin-star kitchen.

## Language

**Chef**:
The human who sets the desired outcome and restrictions, answers decisions, and accepts finished work.

**Supervisor**:
Chef's coordinator, responsible for relaying requests faithfully, organizing workers, and presenting evidence.

**Worker**:
A delegated agent carrying out the task's current scope and reporting its results and blockers.
